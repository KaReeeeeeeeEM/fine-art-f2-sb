Step 3: Prepare the printing surface

Lay your printing surface such as a T-shirt, paper or fabric flat on a clean table. Place cardboard inside the surface to avoid ink bleeding through.

Tape down the edges to prevent movement during printing.

Step 4: Apply the print

(i)

Place the screen frame on top of the printing surface.

(ii)

Make sure the screen is properly centered.

(iii)

Paint the intended image across the top of the screen.

(iv)

Use thick, creamy ink for best results as it is not too runny.

(v)

Pull the squeegee firmly at a 45° angle to push paint through the

screen.

(vi)

Repeat once if needed to ensure full coverage.

(vii) Carefully lift the screen to reveal the print.
